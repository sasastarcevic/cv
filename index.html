  3. Field service

    Field service operations on Salesforce

    Apex · operations

    • Consumed materials waited on a manual ERP step. On finish they go by warehouse, and the document number comes back on the job.
    • Warehouse quantities went stale between visits. A refresh pulls them from the ERP, and the job shows it finished.
    • New equipment is accepted only after the ERP confirms the serial and a second register returns what the unit actually is. The same two checks can be run again later. Leading zeros on a serial no longer split one unit into two records.
    • Customers get a message when the appointment is dispatched, and another when the technician is on the way, each with the link for that moment. Sending stays off the trigger, in a queue.
    • A scheduled job keeps shifts aligned with working days, including deletions for days off. A shift is left alone when the time did not change.
    • For a site-connection request, recipients are chosen by territory and the answer (construction, consent, length, cost, deadline, contacts) exports as one row.
    • Failed outbound calls can be resent for a chosen period, in order, once per job and method, without resetting the job to its start status. Oversized payloads are stored, and logging can be turned off without a release.

    Empty material is an explicit success; errors stay on the job; a reversal uses the same path. A setting keeps the previous integration, so cutover needs no deploy. Apex on the work-order hub. The HTTP audit is the last layer, not the feature.

  4. Digital publishing · subscriptions

    Automatic fiscal invoicing for a multi-market subscription

    Apex · billing

    • Card payments for a regional publisher become fiscal invoices, so finance does not retype each one.
    • Country and plan rules set VAT, currency, and, for a mixed bundle, the split across digital, TV, and print.
    • A returning subscriber is linked to the existing customer in the fiscal system, so name and address are not entered again.
    • The job issues the invoice, emails the customer, and writes the invoice number and public link back to Salesforce.
    • A new plan or a new country is a rule, not a rewrite of the flow.

    Apex, Queueable, per-country strategy, rule engine, dependency injection, HTTP integration with the fiscal API, tests per country and plan.
